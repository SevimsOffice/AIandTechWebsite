## Core Programs (01–09)

### EĞİTİM 01 — Yapay Zeka'ya Giriş ve Claude ile İlk Adımlar
- **Duration**: 4 Saat (Yarım Gün)
- **Level**: Başlangıç
- **Best for**: Any company with zero AI adoption; first-timers
- **Trigger keywords**: no AI usage yet, awareness, starting point
- **Key outcome**: Participants start their first Claude session

### EĞİTİM 02 — Profesyoneller İçin Claude Tam Gün
- **Duration**: 1 Tam Gün
- **Level**: Başlangıç – Orta
- **Best for**: White-collar individual contributors, specialists
- **Trigger keywords**: individual productivity, daily workflow, professionals
- **Key outcome**: Custom Claude Project set up for personal workflow

### EĞİTİM 03 — Takımlar İçin Claude: Operasyonel Dönüşüm
- **Duration**: 2 Tam Gün
- **Level**: Orta
- **Best for**: Teams of 5–20, department managers, collaborative orgs
- **Trigger keywords**: team, department, collaboration, operations
- **Key outcome**: Team Claude ecosystem + Claude Cowork workshop

### EĞİTİM 04 — Üretim ve Mühendislik Yönetiminde Claude
- **Duration**: 1 Tam Gün
- **Level**: Orta
- **Best for**: Manufacturing, production, industrial engineering, quality, maintenance
- **Trigger keywords**: manufacturing, production, factory, engineering, ERP, quality, OEE
- **Key outcome**: 50% reduction in reporting time; ERP data analysis with Claude

### EĞİTİM 05 — Yazılım Ekipleri İçin Claude Code ve Entegrasyon
- **Duration**: 2 Tam Gün
- **Level**: İleri
- **Best for**: Software developers, backend/fullstack engineers, DevOps
- **Trigger keywords**: software, developer, engineering team, DevOps, coding
- **Key outcome**: Claude Code + MCP integration; 60–80% faster routine dev tasks

### EĞİTİM 06 — Karar Vericiler ve Yöneticiler İçin Claude
- **Duration**: 1 Tam Gün
- **Level**: Yönetici
- **Best for**: C-level, department heads, PMO, investment decision-makers
- **Trigger keywords**: executive, CEO, CFO, COO, director, strategy, ROI, board
- **Key outcome**: 6–12 month AI roadmap; ROI measurement framework

### EĞİTİM 07 — Akademik Üretkenlik: Claude ile Araştırma ve Analiz
- **Duration**: 1 Tam Gün
- **Level**: Orta – İleri
- **Best for**: Universities, R&D departments, researchers
- **Trigger keywords**: university, research, academic, R&D, PhD, publication
- **Key outcome**: Literature review halved; personal research assistant with NotebookLM

### EĞİTİM 08 — Claude Skills ve Agentic Akışlarla Süreç Otomasyonu
- **Duration**: 2 Tam Gün
- **Level**: İleri
- **Best for**: IT managers, digital transformation teams, process improvement specialists
- **Trigger keywords**: automation, agentic, digital transformation, IT, process optimization
- **Key outcome**: At least 1 end-to-end workflow automated and live

### EĞİTİM 09 — Claude ile İş Süreçleri Dönüşüm Koçluğu
- **Duration**: 10 Hafta (Haftada 2 saat)
- **Level**: Tüm Seviyeler
- **Best for**: Executives or SME owners wanting deep, sustained transformation
- **Trigger keywords**: coaching, long-term, structured program, KOBİ owner, deep transformation
- **Key outcome**: 5 critical business processes managed autonomously with Claude
